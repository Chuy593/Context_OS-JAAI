Subject: Summary – March 2026 Cost Review

Good afternoon,

Please find below the Executive Summary – March 2026 Cost Review, covering Scrap Yard, Melt Shop, Rolling Mill, and Support Areas.

Scrap Yard
Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $16.14 vs. $15.57 budget → +$0.57 (+4%)
- Total Cost: $930K vs. $918K budget → +$12K (+1%)
- Handled Tons: 57,649 vs. 58,983 budget → -1,334 tons (-2%)

Production & Operational Performance
- Volume below plan by ~1.3K tons, driving fixed cost dilution (+$0.36/ton volume impact) and increasing overall cost/ton

Key Variances
Variable Cost
- Diesel: +$13K (+42%) driven by refueling timing at month-end; no structural increase in consumption

Fixed Cost
- Maintenance: +$30K (+21%) driven by: R&R Scales rental (~$9.7K) not budgeted, Setco tires (~$15.6K) budgeted for January but posted in March (timing issue), SCIApps sensor expense
- Leases: +$44K (+46%) driven by: late invoices from prior months (Sennebogen equipment), lack of monthly accrual process for known recurring lease expenses
- Labor: +$9K (+5%) driven by: overtime linked to headcount gaps and training of new hires, partial bonus posting in March (remaining portion moved to April)
- Support: +$14K (+11%) driven by allocation effects and lower volume

Favorable Offsets
- Scrap Cut: -$58K (-69%) driven by lower cutting activity vs. plan
- Logistics: -$38K (-30%) driven by lower barge unloading and reduced material movement

Melt Shop
Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $244.61 vs. $214.58 budget → +$30.03 (+14%)
- Total Deviation: +$30/ton | +$1.42M
  - Fixed: +$14.97/ton (~$701K)
  - Variable: +$15.06/ton (~$716K)

Production & Yield
- Production: 51,587 vs. 52,200 budget → -613 tons
- Yield: 82.18% vs. 88.50% → -6.32%
- Yield impact: +$28.7/ton (~$1.3M)

Key Variances
Variable Cost
- Electricity: +$5.54/ton (+19%) driven by carryover consumption from previous months
- Process Gases: Oxygen +$1.00/ton (higher consumption and carryover of prior month); Natural Gas +$1.28/ton (driven primarily by higher market prices, +51% vs. budget)
- Refractories: +$5.49/ton (+23%) driven by: ladle trials with new supplier (Coliseum) increasing short-term consumption, delayed February expenses recorded in March, additional tundish and ladle maintenance activities
- S, C & G / Consumables: +$2.12/ton
- Samplers & Lab: +$1.31/ton (+107%)

Fixed Cost
- Maintenance: +$464K (+28%) driven by: outage-related overages (~$400K), emergency spares (motors, cylinders, fittings) not planned or budgeted
- Rentals: +$76K (+131%) driven by: additional equipment required post-outage, late invoices and misclassification under review
- Labor: +$21K (+1%) driven by: overtime (+35%) due to operational instability and headcount gaps, training of new personnel impacting efficiency

Rolling Mill
Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $133.15 vs. $116.53 budget → +$16.62 (+14%)
- Total deviation: +$16.62/ton | +$269K

Production & Yield
- Production: 49,584 vs. 54,345 budget → -4,762 tons (-9%)
- Yield: 93.51% vs. 96.73% → -3.22%
- Yield impact: +$25/ton

Key Variances
Variable Cost
- Electricity: +$3.25/ton (+34%) driven by high energy consumption during downtime (equipment running without production) and frequent changeovers increasing inefficiencies
- Gases (Natural Gas): +$2.92/ton (+36%) driven by market price increase (price-driven, not consumption)

Fixed Cost
- Maintenance: +$274K (+26%) driven by outage-related replacement of two motors (~$348K impact)
