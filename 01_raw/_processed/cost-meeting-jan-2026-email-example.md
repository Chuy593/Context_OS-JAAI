Subject: Cost Meeting - January 2026 - Executive Summary

Good afternoon,

Please find below the Executive Summary – January 2026 Cost Review, covering Scrap Yard, Melt Shop,
Rolling Mill, and Support Areas.

Scrap Yard
Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $18.05 vs. $23.07 budget → ($5.02)/ton | -22%
- Total Cost: $720,080 vs. $967,808 budget → ($247,728) | -26%
- Handled Tons: 39,897 vs. 41,953 budget → (2,056) tons | -5%

Production & Operational Performance
- Handling volume short by ~2,000 tons due to Melt Shop issues

Key Variances
- Diesel: +$5.5K (25%) driven by additional excavator use during downtime (railcar cleaning) and emergency sensor callout.
- All other major categories (Labor, Maintenance, Rentals, Leases, Logistics, Scrap Cut) below budget

Comments (Factual)
- Scrap yard confirmed under-execution is not expected to roll into future months
- Diesel overrun was operational/timing-related; no structural cost concern identified

Melt Shop
Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $265.74 vs. $257.16 budget → +$8.57/ton | +3%
- Total Deviation: $8.57/ton | ($350K)
  - Fixed: +$12.65/ton | +$61K
  - Variable: ($4.18)/ton | ($411K)

Production & Yield
- Production: 34,613 vs. 37,128 budget → (2,515) tons | -7%
- Yield: 82.92% vs. 88.50% budget → -5.58%
- Yield impact: $768,098 unfavorable vs. budget
- Scale miscalibration identified (~1,000 lbs per bucket); correction to be implemented at restart

Key Variances
Variable Cost Drivers
- Samplers & Lab: +$15.5K (+35%), driven by +107% usage vs. standard (partially offset by ~30% price improvement)
- Electricity: Late fee impacted price; without late fee, variable cost would have been under budget by $0.80/ton
- Gases: +$84.4K (+37%)
  - Natural Gas: December invoice analyzed vs. January production; ~10,000 MMBTU variance between invoice and meter readings
  - Oxygen: +$31.3K driven 67% price, 33% consumption; higher low-carbon mix (54% vs. normal 35–40%) increased oxygen demand

Fixed Cost Drivers
- Labor: +$37K due to prepaid February BCBS insurance recorded in January.
- Rentals: +$47K (timing; December/January invoices posted together).
- Support: +$82.6K.
  - General Serv at Plant: $111K baghouse cost misallocated to production instead of outage; to be corrected in February

Comments
- Volume dilution identified as primary driver of fixed cost per ton increase (164 vs. 151 $/ton at budget volume)
- Outage status: restart scheduled Tuesday morning
- Health insurance timing variance confirmed; no February charge expected

Rolling Mill (RM)
Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $190.59 vs. $172.52 budget → +$18.07/ton | +10%
- Total Deviation: $18.07/ton | +$559K
  - Fixed: +$12.00/ton | +$361K
  - Variable: +$6.07/ton | +$198K

Production & Yield
- Production: 33,257 vs. 33,501 budget → (244) tons | -1%
- Yield: 96.97% vs. 96.59% budget → +0.38%
- Yield benefit vs. budget: +$86.9K → +$2.7/ton

Key Variances
Variable Cost Drivers
- Electricity: +$65K (price-driven, late payment fee).
- Gases: +$95.6K; invoice 10,000 MMBTU above meter reading; meter discrepancy under investigation.
- Rolls & Guides: +$37.4K execution variance; full mill reset during outage; not properly budgeted.

Fixed Cost Drivers
- Labor: +$239.7K (maintenance & roll shop; outage window used for additional work).
- Maintenance: +$85.6K (machine shop undulate replacement; motor brush survey).
- Support: +$66.7K.

Comments (Factual)
- Clarified primary RM driver was execution and outage activities, not volume
- Natural gas metering discrepancy under review with Randy

Support Areas
- Total Support Deviation: +$158,934 | +7.74%
  - $103K attributable to prepaid February health insurance.
  - GenServ at Plant: +$111K misallocation (bags charged to cost center instead of outage; to be corrected in February).
  - Storeroom: +$64.7K
    - $41K Prometheus subscription (budgeted August; timing variance)
    - Prometheus subscription to be amortized as asset per accounting discussion
    - $21K freight & customs (gear sets POs 4500239240 & 4500303867)

Overall Plant Summary
- Scrap Yard: Strong cost performance; diesel timing variance only material deviation.
- Melt Shop: Over budget primarily due to volume dilution, insurance timing, gas variances, and misallocations; variable execution favorable overall.
- Rolling Mill: Over budget driven by outage-related labor, maintenance, electricity late fee, and gas discrepancies; volume impact minimal; yield favorable.
- Support: Majority of deviation timing-related (insurance + subscription) and one misallocation to be corrected in February.
