Subject: Cost Meeting - February 2026 - Executive Summary

Good afternoon,

Please find below the Executive Summary – February 2026 Cost Review, covering Scrap Yard, Melt Shop, Rolling Mill, and Support Areas.

Scrap Yard
Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $94.68 vs. $35.09 budget → +$59.59/ton deviation
- Total Cost: $707,424 vs. $865,995 budget → ($158,571) favorable
- Handled Tons: 7,472 vs. 24,678 budget → (17,206) tons deviation

Production & Operational Performance
- Scrap handling activity was significantly reduced due to the 12-day Melt Shop startup delay, which reduced scrap processing volumes during the month.

Key Variances — Main Cost Drivers
- Maintenance: +$63K, mainly related to installation of a new truck scale and fire suppression system not included in the budget.
- Labor: ($75K) favorable due to reduced operating activity.
- Logistics: ($67K) favorable due to lower scrap movements.
- Scrap Cutting: ($50K) favorable due to reduced cutting activity.

Comments
- Scrap Yard spending remained below budget due to lower operational activity.
- Despite lower total spending, cost per ton increased due to fixed cost dilution from lower handled volumes.

Melt Shop
Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $903.86 vs. $378.21 budget → +$525.65/ton deviation
- Production: 5,398 tons vs. 21,840 budget → (16,442) tons deviation
- Yield: 80.38% vs. 88.50% budget → (8.12%) deviation

Key Variances
Variable Cost Drivers
- Electricity: Favorable due to lower operating activity.
- Gases: Favorable due to reduced consumption.
- Refractories and electrodes: Favorable due to significantly lower production.

Fixed Cost Drivers
- Labor: Higher per ton due to fixed workforce during outage.
- Maintenance: Maintenance activities required to restart the plant after the outage.
- Rentals: Incorrect VAC service allocation (~$16K) identified and planned for reversal.
- Support costs diluted due to lower production.

Comments
- Production loss driven by 12-day delayed startup, reducing planned production volume.
- Yield was affected by startup conditions, refractory failures, and tundish losses (~500 tons combined).
- Nitrogen cost increased due to minimum consumption fee applied after low January usage (~30-34% price increase).
- Part of the electricity cost in February includes 1.7 MW adjustment from January invoices, where actual billed demand was higher than the original estimate.

Rolling Mill
Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $122.92 vs. $106.29 budget → +$16.63/ton deviation
- Total Deviation: +$16.63/ton | ($439K) favorable spending
- Production: 39,820 tons vs. 50,177 budget → (10,357) tons deviation
- Yield: 95.28% vs. 96.17% budget → (0.89%) deviation

Key Variances
Variable Cost Drivers
- Electricity: +$26K, higher consumption per ton combined with the electricity contract structure, where lower plant utilization increases the effective cost per kWh.
- Gases: ($85K) favorable due to lower consumption.
- Rolls & Guides: ($124K) favorable due to reduced replacement activity after the outage.
- Tie Wire: +$60K, timing issue between ShipApp and SAP posting.

Fixed Cost Drivers
- Labor: ($56K) favorable.
- Maintenance: ($104K) favorable.
- Support: ($141K) favorable.

Comments
- Production was below plan due to changeovers, coil handling system issues, and downtime events.
- Electricity consumption was estimated using 6,013 MW for the month, while new feeder measurements indicate actual consumption closer to 5,847 MW, currently under validation.
- Due to the electricity contract structure, lower plant utilization increased the effective cost per kWh, contributing to the higher cost per ton during the month.
- Higher cost per ton was mainly driven by fixed cost dilution from lower production volume.

Support Areas
- Total Support Deviation: $1,723,122 vs. $2,058,457 budget → ($335,335) favorable

Major Cost Center Deviations
- Automation: +$26,012, related to installation of the LRF PX3 cabinet, including transport, installation, relocation of cabinets, conduit installation, and reconnection of control and communication cables.
- Billet Yard / Conditioning: +$19,261, freight costs for additional billet purchases required to support Rolling Mill demand.
- Storeroom: +$18,447, freight charges associated with Supplies and Tools purchases not included in the original budget.

Favorable Variances
- Environmental: ($95K)
- GenServ at Plant: ($88K)
- Maintenance Central: ($70K)
- Safety and Quality: Below budget.
