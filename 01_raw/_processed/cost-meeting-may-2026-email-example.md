Subject: Executive Summary – May 2026 Cost Review

Good afternoon,

Please find below the Executive Summary – May 2026 Cost Review, covering Scrap Yard, Melt Shop, Rolling Mill, and Support Areas.

Scrap Yard Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $12.81 vs. $12.69 budget → $0.12/ton unfavorable (+1%)
- Total Cost: $784.6K vs. $948.1K budget → ($163.5K) favorable (-17%)
- Handled Tons: 61,266 vs. 74,712 budget → (13,445) tons (-18%)

Production & Operational Performance
- Handled tons finished below plan due to lower Melt Shop production.
- Volume impact on cost per ton was limited by favorable spending in several cost categories.

Key Variances
- Diesel: +$44.3K | +$0.84/ton due to higher diesel prices versus business plan assumptions.
- Rentals: +$45.4K | +$0.88/ton.
- Maintenance: ($105.2K) | ($1.27)/ton due to deferred activities and spare parts reclassified to inventory.
- Logistics: ($71.2K) | ($0.79)/ton due to lower rail and internal movement activity.
- Scrap Cut: ($66.9K) | ($0.85)/ton.

Comments
- Finance is reviewing diesel consumption and work order allocations.
- Approximately $30K of diesel expense may reverse during June.
- Scrap Yard operated six employees below budgeted headcount and covered shortages through overtime.

Melt Shop Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $207.98 vs. $185.28 budget → $22.70/ton unfavorable (+12%)
- Total Deviation: $22.70/ton | ($762K)
  - Fixed: $17.0/ton
  - Variable: $5.7/ton

Production & Yield
- Production: 55,239 tons vs. 66,120 budget → (10,881) tons (-16%)
- Yield: 85.78% vs. 88.50% budget → (2.72%)
- Yield Impact: $622K unfavorable | $12.7/ton

Key Variances
Variable Cost Drivers
- Electricity: +$3.57/ton due to higher consumption and idle systems remaining operational during production interruptions.
- Gases: ($0.50)/ton favorable due to lower natural gas and argon consumption.
- Refractories: ($2.21)/ton favorable as reline expenses were incurred in April.
- Fluxes: +$2.09/ton driven by higher fluorspar consumption for high-carbon and low-sulfur grades.
- Electrodes: +$1.22/ton due to higher consumption and two LRF electrode break events.

Fixed Cost Drivers
- Maintenance: +$7.52/ton driven by: WSU replacement ($131K), EAF head replacements ($125K), LRF belt replacement ($40K), WSU gearbox replacement ($37K), EAF swing cylinder replacement ($37K)
- Labor: +$5.28/ton driven mainly by overtime and staffing shortages.
- Facility Fee: +$0.69/ton.
- Support: ($0.73)/ton favorable.

Comments
- Production losses were associated with B-3 crane issues, EAF head failures, LRF belt failure, WSU replacement, gearbox replacement, swing cylinder replacement, and caster-related delays.
- Tap-to-tap finished at 96.3 minutes vs. 89.0 budget.
- Yield improved versus prior year but remained below target.
- Holiday payroll allocation will be reflected in June due to payroll timing.

Rolling Mill Cost Performance
- Cost per ton: $104.63 vs. $102.41 budget → $2.22/ton unfavorable (+2%)
- Total Deviation: $2.22/ton | $91K unfavorable

Production & Yield
- Production: 56,891 tons vs. 57,239 budget → (348) tons (-1%)
- Yield: 96.22% vs. 96.82% budget → (0.61%)
- Yield Impact: $231K unfavorable | $4.2/ton

Key Variances
Variable Cost Drivers
- Electricity: +$1.22/ton driven by 17 kWh/ton overconsumption and prior month carryover.
- Gases: ($1.14)/ton favorable due to lower consumption.
- Rolls & Guides: +$0.54/ton.
- Tie Wire: +$0.22/ton due to trial using 1006 tie wire.

Fixed Cost Drivers
- Maintenance: +$5.18/ton driven by: Eddy Current installation ($78K), Vacuum service invoice received after outage ($40K)
- Labor: +$1.42/ton driven by overtime in Roll Shop and Finishing.
- Support: ($0.41)/ton favorable.
- Depreciation: ($4.37)/ton favorable due to projects not yet capitalized.

Comments
- RBO production remained below budget because the operation is one month behind plan.
- 26 product size changes occurred during the month.
- Additional labor costs were incurred for summer heat relief coverage and RBO operator training.
- 401(k) charges were recorded quarterly while budget was phased monthly.

Support Areas
- Total Support Deviation: ($68.2K) favorable for May | YTD +$392K unfavorable (+3.5%)
- Automation: +$12.3K — camera licenses purchased from NextGen; billing delay from vendor caused duplicate monthly charges; intern payroll not budgeted.
- Billet Yard: +$43.1K — billet freight exceeded budget; crane operator resignation resulted in overtime coverage and payroll overage.
- Engineering: ~$400K identified as CAPEX incorrectly charged to OPEX and under correction.
- General Services: $26.8K environmental expense misclassified to General Services; unbudgeted hydraulic fluid and fire-resistant oil purchases; waste collection budget significantly understated.
- Environmental: future months expected to absorb corrected Sabine River Authority charges; fuel surcharges from waste disposal vendor increased due to higher fuel prices.
- Quality Control: +$63.1K — AASHTO audit executed earlier than budgeted; additional external lab testing, software, equipment, and facility repairs; repeated tensile machine repairs exceeded budget.
- Timing / Accounting Effects: billet freight classification under review; multiple freight transactions being charged to expense instead of inventory; ~$400K CAPEX reclassification expected.

Overall Plant Summary
- Scrap Yard: Cost per ton slightly above budget, primarily driven by diesel costs and lower handled tons.
- Melt Shop: Main driver was lower production volume causing fixed cost dilution, with maintenance being the largest operational variance.
- Rolling Mill: Main drivers were maintenance, labor overtime, and electricity consumption.
- Support: Variances driven by freight allocations, timing of invoices, misclassifications, and CAPEX/OPEX accounting corrections.
